I manage my time with lists. Every Sunday I write down everything due that week. Then I sort it by deadline and by how hard it is. Hard tasks go in the morning because that is when I focus best.

Last semester this was tested. I had three assignments and a test in the same week. I also work weekends at a pharmacy. I could not move my shifts, so I had to plan carefully. I broke each assignment into small parts. For example, one day was only for reading, the next day was for the outline. Small parts feel less scary. They also show you quickly if you are behind.

It did not all go to plan. One assignment needed data from a group member who was late. I sent her a message two days before my own deadline. When she still did not reply, I phoned her. She was sick, so I asked our tutor for a short extension. He agreed because I asked early.

What I learnt is that planning is not enough on its own. You must check your plan every day and change it. I also learnt to ask for help sooner. Waiting until the last night only makes things worse. Now I leave one free evening each week for things that go wrong, because something always does.
