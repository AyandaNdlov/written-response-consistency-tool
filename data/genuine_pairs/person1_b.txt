I want to work in human resources because I enjoy the part of work that is about people rather than spreadsheets, even though I've learnt that spreadsheets matter in HR too. During my vac work I spent a lot of time with the HR officer at the depot, and I saw how much of her job was solving problems that nobody else wanted to deal with.

One example was a driver who kept arriving late. His manager wanted to give him a written warning, but the HR officer first sat down with him and found out that his taxi route had changed. She organised a later start time for a month while he sorted out transport. It was a small thing, but it kept a good employee and it showed the other drivers that the company was fair.

I'm also interested in how organisations use data to make decisions about people. In my Industrial Psychology courses we looked at selection tests and how they can be biased if they're not checked properly. I'd like to be part of making those processes fairer, especially in South Africa where access to education has been so unequal.

I know HR can be stressful, because you are often stuck between management and staff. I think I'm suited to that because I'm patient and I'm comfortable having difficult conversations, as long as I have the facts first.
